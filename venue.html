🚗 How to Get Here

Transportation options to reach the Faculty of Medicine

Tanta Railway Station

Tanta is a major railway hub on the Cairo-Alexandria line.

  • From Cairo: ≈2 hours, trains every 1-2 hours
  • From Alexandria: ≈1.5 hours, direct trains available
  • Ticket Price: EGP 50-150 depending on class
From Station to Venue (≈2 km):
  1. Exit Tanta Main Station toward El-Gaish Street
  2. Take taxi from stand outside main entrance
  3. Request "Faculty of Medicine, El-Gaish Street"
  4. Journey: 5-10 minutes, fare ≈ EGP 30-50

Driving to Tanta

Tanta is accessible via the Cairo-Alexandria Agricultural Road.

  • From Cairo: ≈120 km via Agricultural Road (≈1.5-2 hrs)
  • From Alexandria: ≈90 km via Desert Road (≈1-1.5 hrs)
  • Parking: Limited on-site; public parking on El-Gaish St
  • Traffic Tip: Allow extra time during rush hours

Public Transportation

Affordable microbus services connect Tanta with nearby cities.

  • Microbus Routes: Ask for "Al-Bahr" or "El-Gesh" lines
  • Bus Stations: Tanta Central Bus Station has intercity connections
  • Fare: EGP 10-30 from nearby cities
  • Tip: Show venue address to driver for assistance
Getting to Venue:
  1. Disembark at "El-Gaish Street" or "Medical Faculty" stop
  2. Look for Faculty main gate (blue university signage)
  3. Ask security for conference registration desk
  4. Walking time from main road: 2-3 minutes

Language

Arabic is primary. English understood in academic settings. Translation apps helpful.

Currency

Egyptian Pound (EGP). ATMs available. Cash preferred for taxis.

Power

Type C/F outlets (220V). Free WiFi at venue. Bring power bank.

Support

Help desks at venue entrance. Emergency: +20 123 456 7890
